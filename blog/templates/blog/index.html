Welcome to Christianity Post

Shalom!

How are you today? I am so blessed to have you as my visitor. I hope that you will enjoy reading my crafts. It is my desire that you will find inspiration from my writings, and that my blog can help you grow and mature in faith.

The calling of the Lord enabled me to pursue my dream to be a writer. I love to write, and I want this passion to be used in serving the Lord Jesus Christ. I pray that the Holy Spirit will use every piece I make to move your heart to seek our ever-loving God's intervention in your life. God is calling for an intimate relationship with you, my friend. It is by grace that God allowed me to proclaim to the world how good and amazing He is through literature.

Glory, honor, and praise belong to the Holy Father, to the Lord Jesus, and to the Holy Spirit forever! Amen!

May peace and joy from Christ be multiplied to you!
